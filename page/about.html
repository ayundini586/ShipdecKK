About ShipDecKK

Established in 2020 to set new standards in the maritime industry.

Who we are

We specialize in high-end yachts, reliable cargo vessels, and custom-designed ships.

Our success is built on exceptional design, premium materials, and collaboration with skilled craftsmen. Every vessel we offer reflects precision, aesthetics, and long-lasting durability.

As part of our digital transformation, we are developing an online platform to give customers easier access to our products and services.

Our vision

To become a global pioneer in delivering premium maritime solutions by combining unmatched quality, technological innovation, and world-class customer service.

Our mission

  1. Provide high-quality vessels tailored to the unique needs of each customer.
  2. Prioritize service excellence through a personalized and professional purchasing experience.
  3. Innovate continuously by leveraging digital technology to expand our reach and strengthen customer relationships.
  4. Build a sustainable maritime ecosystem by integrating environmentally friendly practices across our operations.

Core values

  1. Excellence. The highest standards in design, materials, and craftsmanship.
  2. Integrity. Trust built through transparency, honesty, and accountability.
  3. Innovation. Progress through technology and creative approaches.
  4. Customer satisfaction. Customer needs and aspirations in every decision.
  5. Traditional craftsmanship. Honoring master shipbuilders as the foundation of our products.

Milestones

2021, Regional recognition

Awarded Best Emerging Maritime Dealer in Asia-Pacific for rapid growth and commitment to vessel quality and customer service.

2022, Expanding client base

Served over 200 international clients, including leading logistics companies and private yacht owners across Asia, Europe, and the Middle East.

2023, Global presence

Opened service centers and showrooms in three international locations, strengthening our after-sales support network.

2024, Digital transformation

Began developing an online platform for browsing vessels, accessing services, and connecting with our team in real time.

Customer satisfaction

A 98% satisfaction rate, driven by quality, transparency, and personalized service.
